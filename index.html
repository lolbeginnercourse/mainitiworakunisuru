What this checks

  • Visible allergens, pork, beef, alcohol, fish, egg, wheat, milk, soy, sesame, soba, and shellfish terms
  • Common hidden risks such as dashi, mirin, cooking sake, soy sauce wheat, broth, batter, gelatin, and shared fryer oil
  • Staff-ready questions and simple order phrases

What it cannot confirm

  • Kitchen procedures, cross-contact, shared utensils, staff mistakes, or unlisted ingredients
  • For severe allergies or strict religious rules, show the staff question before ordering
  • If the photo is unreadable, the scan should fail instead of guessing
