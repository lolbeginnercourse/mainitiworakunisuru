Kagawa — Island Art Route

A compact 2-day Setouchi route for travelers based in Osaka or Kyoto who want art islands, local ferries, small towns and Sanuki udon without overplanning.

  1. Day 1: Travel to Takamatsu, eat udon, check ferry timing, then cross to an art island.
  2. Day 2: Choose one main island, keep the schedule slow, return before the last ferry window feels stressful.
  3. Skip if short on time: Trying to stack too many islands in one day.
